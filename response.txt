**Scene:**  
*Scene opens in a dimly lit sound recording studio. Clara, a seasoned sound recordist, is in the background, listening to a subtle audio track. The room is quiet, save for the soft hum of the studio equipment. She feels a mix of anticipation and tension as she listens to a mix that she never intended to hear, something that whispers through the audio, a note she didn’t think was there.*

**Dialogue:**  
Clara: *“I didn’t expect this to happen. I was focused on my work.”*  
*The sound recordist pauses, then murmurs softly to herself. A low, faint whisper echoes through the room, and she stumbles forward, her heart racing.*

**Sound:**  
*The whisper changes to a voice, softer now, a faint hum in the air—Clara’s own voice.*  

Clara: *“I don’t know what it is… I don’t know. It’s just… something I didn’t think was there.”*  

*She turns back to the studio, the faint sound lingering in the air. The room feels tense, but Clara clutches her pulse, ready to embrace the discovery.*  

**Sound:**  
*The faint hum fades, replaced by a distant note. Clara smiles, her breath catching in her throat, and moves forward, ready to hear something she never thought she would.*  

**End Scene:**  
The scene closes with Clara at the edge of the studio, the faint sound of a whisper echoing in the air, as if it was just her own.
